# Third-party attribution and provenance

`aFold21` is an independently developed terminal wrapper and batch utility. It invokes ColabFold functionality in a remote Google Colab runtime. The ColabFold model code, scientific methods, model parameters, training data, and upstream notebook are not authored by this repository.

## Upstream resources

- Project and source code: https://github.com/sokrypton/ColabFold
- Original notebook: https://github.com/sokrypton/ColabFold/blob/main/AlphaFold2.ipynb
- Open the notebook in Google Colab: https://colab.research.google.com/github/sokrypton/ColabFold/blob/main/AlphaFold2.ipynb
- Primary paper: Mirdita M, Schütze K, Moriwaki Y, Heo L, Ovchinnikov S, Steinegger M. *ColabFold: making protein folding accessible to all.* Nature Methods 19, 679–682 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-022-01488-1
- Upstream license: MIT, https://github.com/sokrypton/ColabFold/blob/main/LICENSE
